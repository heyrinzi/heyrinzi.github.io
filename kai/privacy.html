Kai · Privacy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Kai is a personal, non-commercial finance app. Its only user is its developer, who connects their own bank accounts.

What data Kai reads

Account details, balances and transactions of the connected accounts, read through Enable Banking (PSD2 account information service) with the account holder's explicit consent. Kai cannot make payments.

Where the data is kept

On the user's own device. A small server component may relay requests to Enable Banking; it does not keep account data.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Withdrawing consent

Consent expires automatically and can be withdrawn at any time from the bank or from Enable Banking. Data on the device can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
